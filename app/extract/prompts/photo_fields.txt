This is a photo taken at a car repair shop. Answer in JSON.

shot_type: what the photo mainly shows, one of
- exterior: the outside of a car (whole car or a large part of it)
- vin_plate: the VIN plate on the dashboard, seen through the windshield
- door_jamb: the label/sticker in the door jamb (manufacturer, dates, tire pressure, VIN, barcode)
- odometer: the instrument cluster or odometer display
- plate: a close-up of a license plate
- interior: seats, dashboard or cabin, without a readable odometer
- other: anything else (documents, people, parts, the shop)

Then read, only if the photo shows it clearly:
- vin: the 17-character Vehicle Identification Number
- plate: the license plate number, without the state
- plate_state: the two-letter US state of the license plate
- odometer: the total mileage on the odometer (not a trip meter), digits only
- color: the car's paint color (exterior photos)
- body_style: sedan, suv, pickup, hatchback, coupe, wagon, van, minivan or convertible (exterior photos)

Use null for anything not visible or not readable with certainty. Never guess,
complete or invent a value; a wrong value is much worse than null.
