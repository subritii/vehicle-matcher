{% call ui.details("Show technical details") %}

Invoices: the text layer of digital PDFs (pdfplumber), else PaddleOCR; labels and rules find each field; the VIN must pass its check digit and is decoded with NHTSA vPIC. A local vision model (Ollama, qwen3.5 4B) fills only what is still missing.

Photos: the model sorts each photo (outside, VIN plate, door sticker, odometer, plate); barcodes (zxing-cpp), OCR and a plate reader (fast-alpr) read the details; the model double-checks them.

Matching: photos are grouped per car (shared VIN or plate, or the same phone within 10 minutes). Each group is scored against every invoice. A VIN or plate confirmed by two independent readers on both sides links automatically; a conflicting one rules the invoice out; everything uncertain goes to a person.

OCR and the AI model never run at the same time, so it fits on an 8 GB laptop.
